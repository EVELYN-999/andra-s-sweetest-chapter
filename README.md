# Andra's Sweetest Chapter

Create a hyper-polished, deeply romantic, and interactive proposal web application for a girl named Andra (nickname: "Andra" or "Lessandra"). The visual design must center around a luxurious "Chocolate and Soft Pink" aesthetic: a rich dark cocoa/espresso brown background (#2C1A1D or #3D2326) paired with accents of soft blush pink (#FFB6C1), creamy latte white (#FDFBF7), and glowing rose-gold highlights. Use smooth, modern typography (sans-serif for clean UI, with a delicate serif font like 'Playfair Display' or 'Cormorant Garamond' for romantic headings).

The app should be a single-page experience divided into sequential, smooth-scrolling or tabbed micro-interactions with Framer Motion-style animations. Include the following sections and interactive features:

1. Hero Landing Section:

- A soft, cinematic entry with floating chocolate-colored hearts and glowing pink sparkle particles in the background.

- Main Heading: "For My Favorite Person, Andra 🤎"

- Subheading: "A little corner of the internet just for you..."

- A pulsing, beautifully styled "Open Your Letter 💌" button that smoothly transitions the user into the experience.

2. Interactive "Our Story / Memory Lane" Chapter Cards:

- A horizontal or vertical scroll section featuring 3-4 interactive cute cards representing memories or reasons why she's loved. 

- Each card should have a soft pink background with chocolate borders, cute icons, and a "Tap to flip" or "Click to reveal secret" micro-interaction showing sweet notes.

3. The "Reasons Why I Love You" Interactive Jar:

- A graphic of a cute glass jar filled with tiny pink and chocolate hearts. 

- Every time Andra clicks the jar, a random, deeply sweet, or slightly cheeky reason pops up in an animated floating modal with confetti or heart animations.

4. The Grand Finale Proposal Section:

- A dramatic, beautiful transition where the background deepens into a romantic glowing pink-and-chocolate gradient.

- Central Question: "Andra, will you make me the happiest person and spend forever with me? 💍"

- Two prominent buttons: A massive, glowing "YES! 🥰" button and a playful "No 🙈" button.

- FUN INTERACTIVE FEATURE FOR THE "NO" BUTTON: Every time she tries to hover over or click the "No" button, it playfully dodges or teleports to a different random spot on the screen, growing the "YES" button larger and larger until "YES" takes over the screen.

5. Celebration State (Triggered upon clicking "YES"):

- Confetti explosion (using canvas-confetti) with pink and brown hearts.

- A full-screen overlay featuring a romantic success message: "SHE SAID YES! Best day ever. 🤎✨"

- Playful audio-visual cue placeholder or a sweet note with a countdown or date placeholder.

Technical and UX Requirements:

- Fully responsive, mobile-first design (optimized for when she opens it on her phone via a link).

- Clean component structure using Tailwind CSS and Lucide React icons.

- Extremely smooth transitions, delightful hover states, and zero layout shift.
